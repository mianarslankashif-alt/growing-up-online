2007 – 2026

A history of the internet in America, during my own lifetime

I was born in the United States in 2007 — the same year the iPhone went on sale. This site tracks how the internet changed around me over the two decades since: from the last mile of home broadband, through the smartphone takeover, to streaming, the cloud, the pandemic, and the sudden arrival of generative AI.
